ROLE REFERENCE GUIDE

**The Roles You're Building For**

*Six professional roles \-- one per business category. Each role comes with a description of the person, the company they work for, what they do day-to-day, and an email they sent to a consultant describing their situation. Your job is to read their email and figure out what to build.*

| Note: This is a learning exercise. The companies, people, and situations described in this document are fictional. They were created to give you a realistic and meaningful scenario to build for. The data you will connect to your product next week is a real open-source data from Kaggle, but it has been selected specifically because it matches the type of information someone in this role would actually work with. Use this document to understand your role deeply \-- the company they work for, the problems they face, and why this problem matters to them. |
| :---- |

**Directions**

Your Coach will assign you one of the six roles. Once assigned, do the following:

1. **Read:** Read your role in this document carefully.  
2. **Research:** Use this document as a starting point, but also do your own research. Look up the role title, read about what people in this job actually do, and look up any terminology that is unfamiliar. The more you understand about the role, the stronger your product will be.  
3. **Think:** Think about what their day looks like, what slows them down based on what you’ve read, and what would actually help them \-- not what sounds impressive, but what would make their job easier.
