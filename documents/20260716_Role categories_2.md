**Role 1: Support Specialist**

**Category:** Customer Experience

A Support Specialist works at a technology or software company where customers regularly run into problems and need help. Their job is to respond to customer issues submitted through email, chat, or phone \-- called tickets \-- and resolve them as quickly as possible. At a busy company, a support team might receive hundreds of tickets a week, each one a different customer with a different problem. The Support Specialist has to figure out which ones are most urgent, route them to the right person if needed, and make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

**Company context:**

TechFlow is a software company with about 500 employees that makes tools for small businesses. Customers pay a monthly fee to use the software, and when something goes wrong, they contact the support team directly. The company serves customers across the US and receives around 200 to 300 support tickets every week through email, live chat, and phone. Jordan works as the Support Specialist on a team of four, and is responsible for triaging the incoming queue every morning before the team starts responding.

**Key responsibilities:**

* Reading and responding to customer support tickets across email, chat, and phone channels

* Categorizing and prioritizing incoming tickets based on urgency and issue type

* Tracking open tickets to make sure they are resolved within expected timeframes

* Escalating critical or complex issues to senior team members or the engineering team

* Monitoring customer satisfaction scores and flagging patterns of recurring complaints

* Logging resolution notes so the team can learn from past issues
